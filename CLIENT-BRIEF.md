# Mizuki Booking System — how it works

Thank you for the detailed list of what you need. Everything you asked for is built. Here is a plain-English walkthrough of how it all works, so you can picture it before we go live.

In short: **one system handles the whole journey** — your students see available dates, book, pay, get reminded, and can move their own booking. You stay in control of every session, every place and every course package from one screen.

---

## 1. What your students see

**Step 1 — They open your booking page.**
A calendar shows the next 3 months, so families can plan well ahead. Each date shows a small marker if classes are running that day.

**Step 2 — They click a date.**
All the sessions for that day appear — for example a 10am–12pm Fresh Flower class and a 2pm–6pm Preserved Flower class. Each one shows the time, how long it runs, and how many places are left. Full sessions are clearly marked "Fully booked".

**Step 3 — They book.**
They enter their name, e-mail and contact number. For paid workshops they check out through your existing shop as normal. Their place is held while they pay, so nobody else can take it.

**Step 4 — They get a confirmation straight away.**
An automatic e-mail arrives with the class, date, time and a private link to manage their booking.

**Step 5 — A reminder arrives 2 days before the class.**
Sent automatically. You don't have to do anything.

**Step 6 — They can reschedule themselves.**
Using the link in their e-mail, or by logging into their account. The rules you set decide whether they're allowed — more on that below.

---

## 2. What you see

You get a single **Bookings** menu in your website admin:

| Screen | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Schedule** | Your month at a glance — every session, with how many are booked |
| **Sessions** | Add or change class times, durations and limits |
| **Bookings** | Everyone who has booked; move, cancel or add a student |
| **Course Packages** | IFDA and Preserved Flower students and their remaining sessions |
| **Blocked Dates** | Days the studio is closed |
| **Classes & Rules** | Your rules for each class |
| **Settings** | Reminder timing and e-mail wording |

## 3. Your requirements, one by one

### ✅ 1. Multiple sessions per day

You can run as many sessions in a day as you like — two, three, more. Each one has its own start time and its own length, so a 2-hour Ikebana class and a 4-hour Preserved Flower class can sit on the same day.

You can change any session's time or duration whenever you need to, right up until it runs.

To save you repeating yourself, you can describe the sessions you normally run each week once — "Saturday 10am, 2 hours, Fresh Flower" — and the system fills in the calendar for months ahead automatically. Any individual session can still be edited or cancelled on its own.

### ✅ 2. Participant limit

Every session has a maximum number of students, which you can change at any time.

For students who sign up directly with you through chat, you have two options:

- **Add them as a booking yourself** — takes one of the places, so the website count stays correct and they receive the same confirmation and reminder e-mails.
- **Hold places back** — a simple − button on each session. Press it twice and the website offers 2 fewer places, without changing the real class size. The + button opens places back up, or adds extra ones for a one-off larger group.

The website will never let a session go over its limit.

### ✅ 3. At least 2 months of schedule

The calendar shows 3 months ahead by default, and never less than 2. It extends itself automatically every day, so there is always a full run of dates ahead — you'll never have to remember to "add more dates".

### ✅ 4. Rescheduling

Students can move their own booking from your website, and the rules are different for each class, exactly as you described:

| Class | Can they reschedule? |
|---|---|
| **Preserved Flower** | Yes, freely — up to 24 hours before |
| **IFDA** | Yes, freely — up to 24 hours before |
| **Fresh Flower** | Yes, but **not within 3 days** of the class |
| **Ikebana** | Yes, but **not within 3 days** of the class |

So an Ikebana class on Saturday can no longer be changed from Wednesday onwards — the system stops it automatically and shows a polite message asking them to contact the studio.

We've tested this specific rule carefully, including the exact hours either side of the cut-off, because it's the one that matters most day to day.

These are settings, not fixed code — if you decide 3 days should become 5, it's one number to change.

### ✅ 5. Block out dates

One screen where you mark the days the studio is closed — a single day, or a whole holiday period.

Those dates disappear from the booking calendar immediately, and are skipped when future dates are generated, so nothing can be booked on them. If any students are already booked in that period, you'll be told how many so nobody is left stranded.

### ✅ 6. Course session extension (IFDA & Preserved Flower)

When a student buys a course, the system records their package — for example 25 sessions — and counts down as they book.

You can see at a glance how many sessions each student has used and how many are left. When someone needs more time, you can:

- **Add sessions** — any number
- **Extend the expiry date**
- **Both**

Every extension is recorded with the date, the reason and who made it, so there's always a clear history if a student asks.

If a student buys their course through your shop, their package is created automatically — you don't have to set it up by hand.

### ✅ 7. Auto confirmation after sign-up

Sent the moment they book (or the moment payment goes through, for paid classes). It includes the class, date, time, duration and their private link to manage the booking.

### ✅ 8. Auto reminder 2 days before the class

Sent automatically. You can change it to 1 day or 3 days, and choose what time of day it goes out.

All e-mail wording is yours to edit — you can rewrite any of it in your own voice, and send yourself a test copy before students ever see it.

## 4. What happens automatically, without you

- Confirmation e-mails
- Reminder e-mails 2 days before
- New dates added to the calendar, months ahead
- Closed dates removed from booking
- Places freed up again if someone starts a checkout and doesn't finish
- Course packages created when someone buys a course
- Packages marked complete when all sessions are used

Short list, and we'll handle the technical side:

2. **Any dates you're already closed** — holidays, travel, breaks.

## 6. Two things to decide

**A. Your existing workshop pages.**
At the moment, your workshop products let students pick a date from a simple dropdown list. That list doesn't know how many people have already booked, so in theory a class could be overbooked. Moving those products across to the new system fixes that. We'll do them one at a time and test each before switching over, so nothing goes down.

**B. The IFDA membership platform.**
What's built already gives IFDA students their own login where they can see their remaining sessions, their upcoming classes, and book or reschedule.

If "membership platform" means more than that to you — for example course materials to download, certificates, progress tracking or instructor sign-off — that's a separate piece of work. Could you tell us in a little more detail what you'd like your IFDA students to be able to do when they log in? Then we can plan it properly.
